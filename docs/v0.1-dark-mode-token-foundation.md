## Audited values adjusted for contrast

WCAG relative luminance uses linearized sRGB channels (threshold 0.04045) and `(Llighter + .05)/(Ldarker + .05)`. Decisions use unrounded values; ratios below are rounded to two decimals. Target normal text 4.5:1; applicable control boundaries/focus 3:1. These calculations do not establish formal accessibility conformance.

- Subtle `#8D82A0` gives 4.45:1 on secondary and 4.08:1 on raised aubergine. The token serves small guest badges and placeholders, not just decorative icons. `#A99CBD` gives 6.23:1 and 5.72:1.
- Control boundary `#7862A0` gives 2.84:1 against the raised/popover surface. The shared secondary button/control boundary contract must work on that surrounding surface too. `#8C819C` gives 4.01:1 (4.85:1 against its normal input fill).
- Pressed accent `#8B5CF6` gives only 4.49:1 with the required dark on-accent text. `#B79BFA` gives 8.23:1.
- Disabled `#8A7E9F` gives 4.37:1 on the audited disabled surface. `#A99CBD` gives 6.42:1, preserving readability without opacity. Disabled controls are generally contrast-exempt; this is a readability choice.
- Audited destructive active `#DC2626` gives 3.94:1 with dark on-accent; the pre-existing literal `#7F1D1D` gives only 1.90:1. Dark pressed `#E84040` gives 4.75:1. Audited hover `#EF4444` already gives 5.05:1 and is retained.
